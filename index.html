The story

I learned how houses should be built. Then I bought one.

I grew up an independent home builder's kid. Job sites were the family business, and I learned early what right looks like: square corners, honest schedules, work you'd sign your name to. I know how things should be made.

Then I bought my own home and spent ten agonizing years trying to get renovations completed. On budget. On time. Done right the first time. It didn't happen, for more reasons than I can count, and I had every advantage: I knew the trades, I knew the language, I knew what good work looked like. It still went sideways.

That's why Wavelength Home exists. Not because I read about the problem, but because I lived it, and nobody who's paying for a build should have to learn these lessons at that price.

The stance

Oversight, not contracting.

The construction industry works fine for everyone except the person paying for it. Contractors have estimators, project managers, and lawyers. Homeowners have a gut feeling and a Google search. We close that gap with someone who has read the contract, walked the site, and seen every trick before.

Independent by design

No kickbacks, no referral fees, no preferred-vendor lists. Our only revenue is your fee, so our only loyalty is your outcome.

Fluent in both languages

We translate builder-speak into decisions you can actually make, and homeowner priorities into terms crews respect.

There before the problem

Most construction disputes are lost at signing, not at framing. We get involved before the contract, not after the leak.

Six ways in

Every phase of the build has a version of us.

Pre-Build Planning

Scope, budget reality-check, and sequencing before anyone bids. The cheapest change order is the one that never happens.

Bid & Contract Review

Line-by-line review of estimates and agreements. We flag the allowances, exclusions, and clauses that cost people houses.

Construction Oversight

Scheduled site walks at the milestones that matter, with photo documentation and plain-English progress reports.

Punch List & Closeout

The final ten percent is where quality goes to die. We build the punch list, verify the fixes, and clear the final payment.

Renovation Advisory

Thinking about opening that wall? A one-time consult on feasibility, cost ranges, and what your house will actually allow.

Issue Resolution

When a project has already gone sideways: independent assessment, documentation, and a path back to done.
